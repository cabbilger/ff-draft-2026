How market values & inflation are calculated

Base value is now anchored to this league's own real auction history, not just external market blends. We pulled the actual ESPN Draft Recap prices for every pick in the 2024 and 2025 seasons (420 picks total), excluded the one keeper pick per team per year (each team gets to retain a player from the prior year's roster at last year's price, which isn't a real market bid), and used the remaining 409 clean picks as ground truth:

  • Real per-position budget shares (2-year average, clean picks only): WR 36.4%, RB 35.7%, QB 19.0%, TE 5.2%, LB 1.6%, DL 0.7%, DST 0.5%, DB 0.5%, K 0.4%. Every position's dollar total in the pool is rescaled so the full player pool balances against these real shares of the $3,000 league budget (10 teams × $300), instead of a generic external blend.
  • Any player who actually appeared in one of those 420 real picks has their base value overridden with their real weighted price (65% weight on their 2025 price, 35% on 2024, when both exist) — 229 players in the current pool got a real-price override this way. Everyone else (players new to the league, or who didn't appear in the last two drafts) keeps a value from the rescaled model above.
  • QB: real data shows QB1 topping out around $65–69 (Josh Allen, Lamar Jackson) even with the OP utility slot in play — not the $90–98 Superflex-style premium an earlier version of this sheet assumed. The OP slot does add some lift over a standard single-QB league, but this league's real bidding never treated it like true Superflex.
  • DL/LB/DB/DST (IDP) turned out to be the biggest correction: an earlier version of this sheet assumed IDP/DST combined for ~24% of budget share, on the theory that 5 IDP-eligible slots (DL, LB, DB, 2×DP flex) would drive real premiums. Real history says otherwise — combined IDP is only ~2.8% of actual spend, and DST just ~0.5%. Actual per-position stats: DL avg $1.40/max $6, LB avg $2.07/max $7, DB avg $1.35/max $5 (Travis Hunter, who's dual-eligible at WR and DB in this pool, is the one real outlier at $15), DST avg $1.48/max $4. This league is not paying an IDP premium — if anything it's routinely leaving IDP/DST on the table for $1.

Hover any "Base $" cell (or check the Bid Assistant panel once a player is selected) to see the player's value; players with a real-history override are priced directly from what this league actually paid for them the last two years.

Personal spend caps (My Strategy panel): DL/LB/DB/DST default to a personal ceiling ($5 for DL/DB, $6 for LB, $4 for DST) set just above the real historical max for each position — not because this league overbids IDP (it doesn't), but as a guardrail against getting drawn into the rare outlier bidding war. The Bid Assistant will flag PASS the moment a bid would cross that number for your team. Edit or remove the caps any time.

Mock draft opponent AI: Mock Draft mode auto-simulates the whole draft, and all 10 teams (including your own seat) now bid using personality profiles built from each real manager's own 2024–2025 bidding history — which positions they actually paid up for relative to the league average (posAffinity), whether they tend to win bidding wars or land bargains (aggression), and whether they concentrate spend on 1–2 studs or spread it evenly (starHunter). This also drives which slot each team tends to nominate for. It's a real-data-informed heuristic, not a prediction — treat mock draft results as a plausible rehearsal, not a forecast of what your actual league mates will do.

FF Rank column: positional ranks from The Fantasy Footballers' 2026 Draft Rankings (Andy/Jason/Mike/Clips consensus) are shown alongside for QB/RB/WR/TE as an independent cross-check — these ranks don't feed into the $ value math at all, they're purely a second opinion to sanity-check against. When it and our $ model disagree by 8+ spots within a position, you'll see a ▲/▼ badge: an up-arrow (green) means the Fantasy Footballers rank the player notably better than our value model does (possible buy-low), a down-arrow (orange) means the opposite (possible overvalued by our model). ~60 deep bench/waiver players that only appeared in the Fantasy Footballers list (not ESPN's top 300) were added to the pool at a flat $1 floor value.

Live inflation recalculates as picks are logged: multiplier = (money left in the league − $1×slots left) / (base value left in the pool − $1×slots left). If teams are overspending vs. the sheet, the multiplier rises above 1.0 and every remaining player's target price is bumped up accordingly (and vice versa). This is the standard auction-inflation formula used by most auction tools.

Treat base $ values as a reasonable starting anchor, not gospel — real rooms deviate. Keeper picks were excluded from the historical calibration using the league's actual keeper announcements (18 of the 20 team-year keepers across 2024–2025 confirmed by name and price; NUTZ's and TB12's 2024 keeper couldn't be pinned down from the record and were left in, so those two team-years may carry a small amount of keeper-discount noise). The live inflation adjustment and your personal caps are what keep recommendations honest as your specific draft unfolds.

Cloud sync: every logged pick, undo, or cap change is automatically saved to a free Firebase database in the background (see the status line under Log Pick / Export). If the page crashes or you reload, it reloads your draft-in-progress automatically instead of starting blank. Export/Import JSON still work exactly as before as a manual backup/portability option.
